## Comeback QB

# What it is

Joe was one of the greatest quarterbacks in football, at least, until he tore his ACL, MCL, PCL, LCL, both menisci, and approximately 47 other body parts.
Joe now is facing the biggest challenge of his career. His competitive spirit doesn't allow a small thing like injuries to shape his decisions.
He needs to win. The scene is set. One last drive, one last play.
Will Joe come back and win?

# How to play
Pass the ball to a receiver labelled 1-5, before you get sacked (4.5 seconds) and use WASD keys to control the ballcarriers and take them into the endzone.
